Our Community Mission

We created this community to provide a judgment-free zone where plus-size women can support each other on their health journeys. Unlike other fitness groups, we focus on encouragement, understanding, and real-life experiences without any pressure or competition. Notre engagement est de créer un espace inclusif et empowering.

  • No body shaming—ever.
  • No toxic comparisons; everyone's path is unique.
  • No competition; we lift each other up.
  • Mutual support through shared stories and advice.
  • Confidentiality respected to create a safe space.
  • Inclusivité pour toutes les morphologies et origines.

Safety & Confidentiality

Your privacy is our priority. Nous appliquons les standards les plus élevés pour protéger vos données et assurer un environnement sécurisé. Voici comment nous procédons :

  • Private space accessible only to members, with verified accounts.
  • Strict data privacy compliance (GDPR and HIPAA-inspired standards).
  • Active moderation 24/7 to ensure positivity and remove harmful content.
  • Clear rules promoting respect, kindness, and zero tolerance for harassment.
  • Options for anonymous posting to encourage open sharing.
  • Easy reporting system for any concerns, with quick response times.
  • Regular audits and security updates to protect your information.
