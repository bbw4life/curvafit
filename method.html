The 3 Pillars

The Exact Rules of Each Pillar

Not vague principles. Specific, actionable rules — with the science behind each one clearly explained.

01

Nutrition 70% of Results

The engine. No exercise compensates for poor nutrition. Get this right and fat loss becomes inevitable, predictable, and sustainable.

  • ✓
    300–500 cal deficit/day. Below 300 = too slow. Above 500 = muscle loss and famine mode. This range is the medically confirmed fat-burning sweet spot.
  • ✓
    0.8–1.2g protein per kg body weight. A 90 kg woman needs 72–108g daily, split across 3 meals. Without this, you lose muscle, not just fat.
  • ✓
    Eliminate liquid calories first. 1 soda + 1 juice + 1 sweetened coffee = ~460 cal — your entire deficit — with zero satiety.
  • ✓
    3 structured meals, no unplanned snacking. Each unplanned snack averages 200–300 cal. Two per day wipes out your deficit entirely.
  • ✓
    2 liters of water daily. Suppresses false hunger and increases calorie burn by 24–30% for 60–90 min after drinking.
02

Movement 20% of Results

The multiplier. Movement alone won't produce major fat loss — but combined with nutrition, it accelerates results and prevents metabolic slowdown.

  • ✓
    30-min brisk walk = 150–250 cal burned daily. Over 30 days = 4,500–7,500 extra calories = 0.5–1 kg of additional fat loss. No gym needed.
  • ✓
    Strength training 3×/week preserves muscle. Every 0.5 kg of muscle burns 6–10 extra cal/day at rest — compounding over 12 months.
  • ✓
    Low-impact only for weeks 1–8. Running puts 3–5× your body weight on joints. Floor exercises deliver the same effect without injury risk.
  • ✓
    Movement reduces cortisol by 15–20%. Since cortisol signals fat cells to store energy, exercise is a direct fat-loss mechanism beyond calories.
  • ✓
    Daily 30-min beats weekly 2-hour sessions. 30 min × 30 days = 900 min vs. 2h × 4 weeks = 480 min. Consistency doubles total output.
03

Mindset Hidden Multiplier

Ignored by 95% of programs. But mindset directly controls two fat-storage hormones — getting this wrong kills results even when nutrition and movement are perfect.

  • ✓
    Cortisol management is a fat-loss strategy. Chronic stress → cortisol → abdominal fat storage. Reducing stress is a direct metabolic intervention.
  • ✓
    Sleep 7–8 hours controls hunger hormones. Poor sleep raises ghrelin +28% and drops leptin — creating biologically uncontrollable cravings.
  • ✓
    Body shame increases cortisol. Criticizing your body raises cortisol as effectively as physical threats. Self-compassion is a measurable strategy.
  • ✓
    Track 4 metrics, not just weight. Log waist, energy (1–10), sleep hours, and exercise completion — they show real progress when scale fluctuates.
  • ✓
    Community support = +65% success rate. Women with peer accountability are 65% more likely to maintain weight loss for 12+ months.

Myth vs Science

5 Myths That Keep Plus-Size Women
Trapped in Failure Cycles

These beliefs feel completely true. The biology says otherwise — and biology always wins.

Myth
01

"Eating almost nothing is the fastest way to lose weight"

✦
Science

Eating under 1,200 cal/day triggers adaptive thermogenesis — your metabolic rate drops up to 30% within 2 weeks. Fat loss stops. The moment you eat normally again, you regain faster. The 300–500 cal deficit is the clinically proven sweet spot.

Myth
02

"You need intense daily cardio to lose significant weight"

✦
Science

Intense cardio spikes cortisol, raises hunger the next day, and increases injury risk — causing most people to quit within 3 weeks. A 30-min brisk walk burns equivalent relative fat, keeps cortisol low, and has a near-zero dropout rate.

Myth
03

"Diet pills and detox teas produce real fat loss"

✦
Science

Most OTC slimming pills are stimulants that raise heart rate temporarily. They don't break down stored fat. Detox teas are laxatives causing water loss, not fat loss. Neither creates a calorie deficit — and both cause rebound when stopped.

Myth
04

"Losing weight means losing your curves forever"

✦
Science

Fat loss with adequate protein and strength training preserves muscle and often improves hip-to-waist ratio. The result is a smaller, stronger version of your existing feminine shape — not a loss of identity. Your curves are structural, not just volumetric.

Myth
05

"My genetics make it biologically impossible to lose weight"

✦
Science

Genetics influence where fat is stored and the rate of loss — but they cannot override a sustained calorie deficit. Studies of identical twins confirm fat loss occurs in all genetic profiles. Genetics set the difficulty level, not the possibility.

If your exercise performance decreases week over week, increase protein. If the scale drops but measurements stay the same or increase, you may be losing muscle — a sign of too large a deficit or insufficient protein.

A plateau occurs when adaptive thermogenesis reduces your metabolic rate — you now burn fewer calories at the same deficit. Three effective responses: (1) Add 10 minutes to daily walks. (2) Take a 7–10 day "diet break" at maintenance calories to reset metabolic rate upward. (3) Audit your actual food intake — portion creep is the hidden cause of most plateaus. Yo-yo happens when people abruptly stop their deficit and return to old habits — metabolism was adapted to lower calorie intake, causing rapid regain. CurvaFit's maintenance protocol prevents this by gradually increasing calories +100/week over 4–6 weeks.
