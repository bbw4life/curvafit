The Origin

Born from a Moment That
Changed Everything

"What if I could help these people regain confidence — without suffering, without pressure — simply by starting at home, at their own pace?"

— Paul Francenel, Founder

CurvaFit was born on November 5, 2025 — from a simple but powerful moment. Paul Francenel, a 25-year-old entrepreneur passionate about sport and personal transformation, was walking in the street with friends when he noticed a woman in difficulty, struggling with every step because of her weight. That scene touched him deeply.

He understood that for many women, going to a gym is intimidating, distance or lack of money is a barrier, and the fear of judgment blocks all motivation.

That is the moment CurvaFit was born. Not from a business plan — from a human observation. Paul is not a doctor or a fitness coach — and that is exactly what allows him to understand a reality that professionals often miss: the reality of people who want to change but do not know where to start.

Built for Your Biology

Designed Around Plus-Size Physiology

Plus-size women are not simply larger versions of average-sized women. Their bodies have specific hormonal profiles, metabolic patterns, and physiological realities that require a different approach entirely.

Insulin sensitivity tends to be lower in heavier bodies — meaning carbohydrate timing and protein prioritization matter more. Estrogen levels influence fat distribution in ways that generic programs never account for. Cortisol, elevated by chronic stress and by shame itself, directly promotes abdominal fat storage.

CurvaFit was built with all of this in mind. Every exercise was chosen to protect the joints that carry more weight. Every meal structure was designed to control insulin response and maximize satiety. Every protocol — sleep, hydration, stress management — addresses the specific hormonal environment of plus-size bodies.
