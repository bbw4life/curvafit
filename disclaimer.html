Medical & Fitness Disclaimer

At CurvaFit, your safety and trust are everything. This page tells you exactly what we are, what we are not, and what you need to know before starting. No fine print hidden.

Last updated: March 20, 2026 Version 2.0 Questions:

What CurvaFit IS

  • A lifestyle education program based on science
  • Structured guidance on nutrition, movement, hydration, and sleep
  • A support community for plus-size women on a weight loss journey
  • Low-impact home workout suggestions adapted to larger bodies
  • General calorie and protein education to help you understand fat loss
  • A safe, judgment-free space that respects your pace and your body

What CurvaFit IS NOT

  • Not a medical clinic, hospital, or licensed healthcare provider
  • Not a substitute for advice from your doctor or nutritionist
  • Not a treatment for any disease — including diabetes, PCOS, or thyroid conditions
  • Not a personalized medical nutrition therapy service
  • Not a guarantee of specific weight loss results for any individual
  • Not affiliated with or a replacement for any pharmaceutical product

1. General Information

All content on the CurvaFit website — including articles, programs, workout guides, meal plans, and community resources — is provided for educational and informational purposes only. This information does not constitute professional advice of any kind.

You are fully responsible for how you interpret and apply the information. CurvaFit makes no warranties about the completeness, accuracy, or suitability of any content for your personal situation.

2. Medical Disclaimer

CurvaFit does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment of any kind. Nothing on this website, in our programs, or in our community should be interpreted as medical guidance.

Always consult a licensed healthcare professional before starting any fitness or nutrition program — especially if you have or suspect any of the following conditions:

  • PCOS (Polycystic Ovary Syndrome) or other hormonal conditions
  • Type 1 or Type 2 diabetes
  • Thyroid disorders (hypothyroidism, hyperthyroidism)
  • Cardiovascular disease or high blood pressure
  • Chronic joint pain, arthritis, or orthopedic conditions
  • History of eating disorders
  • Any condition requiring medication that affects metabolism or weight

If your doctor has placed specific dietary or exercise restrictions on you, those instructions always take priority over anything suggested in CurvaFit programs.

3. Pregnancy & Breastfeeding

CurvaFit programs are designed for non-pregnant adults. If you are pregnant, recently postpartum, or breastfeeding, you must consult your obstetrician or midwife before following any exercise or nutrition guidance.

Weight loss during pregnancy is not recommended. Calorie restriction during breastfeeding can affect milk supply. Your health and your baby's health always come first.

4. Fitness & Exercise Disclaimer

All exercise suggestions on CurvaFit are low-impact movements designed with plus-size bodies in mind. However, all physical activity carries inherent risk, regardless of intensity.

Before exercising, please follow these safety principles:

  • Stop immediately if you experience chest pain, dizziness, shortness of breath, or sharp joint pain
  • Warm up before any session
  • Listen to your body — discomfort from effort is normal; pain from injury is not
  • Modify movements to your current fitness level
  • Rest when needed — rest days are part of the method

CurvaFit does not recommend high-impact exercises for women just beginning or with joint conditions. This is a protection, not a limitation.

5. Nutrition Disclaimer

All nutritional guidance on CurvaFit — including meal plans, calorie estimates, protein targets, and food suggestions — is general educational information only. It is not personalized medical nutrition therapy.

Calorie and macronutrient values are estimates based on general population averages. Your actual needs may vary based on your height, weight, age, activity level, and medical history.

If you have specific dietary needs due to a medical condition, allergies, or medication, always work with a registered dietitian.

CurvaFit does not promote extreme restriction, crash dieting, meal replacement products, or unsafe fasting protocols.

6. Results Disclaimer

We are honest about weight loss because we respect you. Here is the truth:

Safe Rate

A realistic, safe fat loss rate for plus-size women is 0.5 to 1 kg per week, or 2–4 kg per month. Faster is not better — it often means muscle loss.

Individual Variation

Results depend on your starting weight, age, hormonal health, sleep, stress, and consistency. No two bodies are the same.

No Guarantees

CurvaFit does not guarantee any specific weight loss outcome. Any number mentioned represents a possible range, not a promise.

Time Required

Sustainable results take months, not days. Anyone promising dramatic results in one or two weeks is misleading you. We will not do that.

7. Testimonials Disclaimer

Success stories and testimonials represent the real, individual experiences of specific members. They are not fabricated, but they are also not typical results for every member.

A woman who lost 24 kg in 7 months followed the program with high consistency and had no major medical obstacles. Her result is inspiring and real — but not guaranteed for every woman.

We show these stories because they demonstrate what is possible, not what is automatic.

8. Supplements & Weight Loss Pills

CurvaFit does not recommend, endorse, or sell weight loss pills, detox teas, appetite suppressants, or any unregulated supplement as a weight loss tool.

Most over-the-counter slimming products are unregulated, have no proven efficacy for sustained fat loss, and can carry serious risks.

If any product in the CurvaFit Shop mentions supplementation, it refers only to standard nutritional support (such as vitamin D or magnesium) and must be discussed with your healthcare provider.

If any third party claims to sell "CurvaFit pills" or "CurvaFit supplements," this is not affiliated with us. Please report it to immediately.
