Know Your Body

The Truth About Losing Weight
as a Plus-Size Woman

Most programs were not built for your body. Here is what science actually says.

Safe Weight Loss Rate

A plus-size woman can safely lose 0.5 to 1 kg per week. This medically proven range preserves muscle and protects your hormones. Faster = damage.

Calorie Deficit — The Real Key

Weight loss happens when you eat 300–500 fewer calories than you burn daily. Not zero carbs, not extreme fasting — just a moderate, sustainable deficit.

Cortisol & Stress Make You Gain

Stress releases cortisol, which signals your body to store fat — especially belly fat. Harsh diets increase cortisol. CurvaFit reduces it.

Diet Pills Are a Lie

Most slimming pills are unregulated, damage your liver, and cause rebound weight gain. No pill replaces food discipline and movement.

Why Yo-Yo Dieting Happens

Cutting too many calories triggers "famine mode" — your metabolism slows and stores more fat once you eat normally again. That's the yo-yo cycle.

You Can Do This at Home

80% of weight loss comes from what you eat. You do not need a gym. Structured home routines adapted to your body are enough.

Science Explains It

Why 95% of Diets Fail Plus-Size Women

These are biological facts that most programs completely ignore.

  • 01
    Famine Mode (Adaptive Thermogenesis) Drastic calorie cuts lower your metabolic rate by up to 30%. You burn fewer calories even at rest — making weight loss stop completely after a few weeks.
  • 02
    Muscle Loss Without Protein Low-calorie diets without sufficient protein cause your body to break down muscle for energy. Less muscle = slower metabolism = harder to lose fat long-term.
  • 03
    Cortisol Sabotage Chronic dieting stress raises cortisol, which signals fat cells — especially abdominal ones — to store more energy. The more you stress about your weight, the more your body fights back.
  • 04
    Sleep Deprivation Raises Hunger Hormones Less than 7 hours of sleep increases ghrelin (hunger) by 28% and decreases leptin (fullness). You wake up ravenous — no diet can override that biology.
  • 05
    Wrong Exercises for Plus-Size Bodies High-impact cardio puts 3–5x your body weight on your joints, causing inflammation and pain. Low-impact alternatives burn the same calories without the damage.

Nutrition Rules

The 5 Food Rules That Make the Difference

  • Protein at every meal — chicken, eggs, fish, legumes. Keeps you full and preserves muscle
  • Cut liquid sugars first — sodas, juices, sweetened coffee. One change that saves 300–500 calories daily

  • 2 liters of water daily — reduces bloating, false hunger, and boosts fat metabolism
  • Eat until 80% full — your satiety signal takes 20 minutes to reach your brain. Stop before you feel stuffed
