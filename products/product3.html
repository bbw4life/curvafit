How to Adjust the Cable Length
+

Step 1: Hold the rope handles and stand on the center of the cable.

Step 2: The handles should reach your armpits when pulled up — this is the ideal length.

Step 3: Unscrew the handle cap, slide the cable to your desired length, re-tighten the cap. Done in under 30 seconds!

How accurate is the jump counter?
›
The precision magnetic sensor in each handle detects 99.9% of jumps accurately — even at very high speeds. The counter auto-resets at 9,999 and continues tracking, so there's no limit to how many reps you can count.
Can I use it on hard floors?
›
Yes! The steel cable is coated in PVC for durability on any surface — hardwood, tile, concrete, or outdoors. For extra protection of your floors, we recommend a jump mat for wooden surfaces.
How many calories does it actually burn?
›
Jump rope burns 700–900 calories per hour — more than running, cycling, or swimming at the same intensity. The Smart Jump Rope calculates your burn based on your jump speed and weight setting.
Is it good for beginners?
›
Absolutely. Start with sets of 30 jumps with rest in between. Within 2 weeks most beginners can do 3 minutes non-stop. The counter makes it easy to set micro-goals and track progress session by session.
What battery does it use?
›
It uses 2× AAA batteries (included in the box). Battery life is approximately 6–12 months depending on frequency of use. Standard batteries available everywhere.
