Smart Hula Hoop — Size Guide & Assembly Instructions

The Smart Hula Hoop is fully modular — simply add or remove sections to match your exact waist size. Use a soft measuring tape around your natural waist (just above the navel) to find your size. Measurements are provided in both inches (in) and centimeters (cm).

📏 How to Measure Your Waist

  • Step 1: Stand upright, breathe out naturally and relax your stomach.
  • Step 2: Wrap the tape around your natural waistline — the narrowest point, usually just above your navel.
  • Step 3: Keep the tape flat and parallel to the floor. Don't suck in or pull too tight.
  • If between two sizes, always choose the larger size for comfort during spinning.
  • You can buy extra sections separately and adjust for different family members anytime.

📐 Size Chart — Sections vs Waist

Size Sections Max Waist (cm) Max Waist (inches) Best For
Small18 sectionsUp to 89 cmUp to 35"Slim & average build
Medium21 sectionsUp to 100 cmUp to 39"Average to curvy
Large24 sectionsUp to 130 cmUp to 51"Plus size & curvy figures

🔧 How to Assemble the Hoop (2 minutes, no tools)

  • Step 1: Lay all sections out on a flat surface and count them.
  • Step 2: Snap sections together one by one — each piece clicks securely into the next. You'll hear a click when properly locked.
  • Step 3: Connect the last section back to the first to form a complete circle.
  • Step 4: Step into the hoop, position it at your natural waist level, and start with a gentle side-to-side hip motion.
  • 💡 Tip: If the hoop feels too large, remove 1–2 sections. If too tight, add more sections.

How do I choose the right number of sections?
›
Measure your natural waist with a soft tape. Choose 18 sections for waist under 89 cm (35"), 21 sections for under 100 cm (39"), and 24 sections for up to 130 cm (51"). When in doubt, go one size larger. Check our full size chart above for details.
How long before I see results?

Most users notice visible waist changes within 2–4 weeks of consistent daily use (10–15 min/session). The hoop activates the oblique and core muscles directly, which shapes your waistline over time. Results vary based on diet and overall activity level.
Is it safe for post-natal recovery?
›
Yes — the Smart Hula Hoop is a low-impact tool, ideal for gentle post-natal core recovery. Start with 5-minute sessions and gradually increase. Always consult your physician first, especially after a C-section or complex delivery.
Can children use it safely?

Yes! Simply set the hoop to a smaller size (fewer sections) for a child's waist. It's a safe, fun and active way to keep kids moving. Adult supervision is recommended for young children.
