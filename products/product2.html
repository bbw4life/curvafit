Power Waist Trainer — Size Guide

Measure around your natural waist (just above your navel) and your hip circumference. When between sizes, go one size up for day wear, or stay for maximum compression.

How to Measure

  • Step 1: Stand upright, breathe out naturally.
  • Step 2: Wrap the tape around your natural waistline — just above your navel.
  • Step 3: Keep the tape parallel to the floor. Note the measurement in inches or cm.
  • Tip: Also measure your hips (widest point) to ensure hip fit.

›
Yes. The neoprene generates infrared heat that increases sweating by 3× at the waist, breaking down fat cells faster. Combined with daily wear, most customers lose 3–8 cm in the first month of consistent use.
Can I wear it all day at the office?
›
Absolutely. The slim profile is completely invisible under business attire, dresses, or jeans. The soft inner lining keeps you comfortable for 8+ hours without irritation.
Is it safe for postpartum recovery?
›
Yes — it's widely recommended for postpartum core recovery. It helps support weakened abdominal muscles and encourages the uterus to return to its original position. Always consult your physician before starting any compression garment after giving birth.
How do I choose between Fuchsia and Black?
›
Black is your everyday choice — invisible under most clothing and infinitely versatile. Fuchsia is for when you want to feel bold and empowered — perfect for gym sessions where you want your waist trainer seen.
How tight should I wear it?
›
Snug but never painful. You should be able to breathe normally and move freely. Start at a comfortable tension and gradually increase compression over 1–2 weeks as your body adapts. Never wear it so tight that it restricts breathing.
How long until I see results?
›
Instantly when you put it on — your silhouette changes in 5 seconds. For permanent results, most customers see a noticeable waist reduction after 4 weeks of daily use (6–8 hours/day).
