What is inside the 15-page guide?
+

It includes a 14-day restart, three restart rules, prompts to understand a setback and identify triggers, a five-minute Plan B, kind self-talk examples, common-obstacle responses, a personal safety net, and a final check-in.

Is this a workout plan?
+

No. It is a general guide to rebuilding a routine with small actions such as a short walk, gentle stretching, drinking water, or taking a breathing pause. Choose what feels manageable and listen to your body.

What if I miss a day during the 14 days?
+

A missed day does not erase your effort. The guide encourages you to begin smaller, avoid punishment or catch-up plans, and return with one manageable action when you are ready.

What does the 14-day restart include?
›

Daily reflection themes, trigger planning, a five-minute Plan B, kind self-talk prompts, ideas for common obstacles, and a check-in to choose what you want to keep.

Do I have to follow a strict daily schedule?
›

No. The guide offers one small theme for each day, but it is meant to support a flexible return to routine. Start smaller than you think you need to and move at a pace that feels manageable.

What is the five-minute Plan B?
›

It is a simple response for a difficult day: pause, choose one tiny action for five minutes, then finish without guilt. Examples include a short walk, gentle stretches, preparing clothes, drinking water, or breathing quietly.

Is this a substitute for medical advice?
›

No. This is a general routine and self-kindness guide, not medical advice. Consult a healthcare professional if you have questions about what is appropriate for your health.
