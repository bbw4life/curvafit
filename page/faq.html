About the guides

What are the Curvafit guides?

They are practical digital PDFs designed to help you create routines with more structure and less pressure:

  • Gentle Walking is a four-week walking guide with weekly plans, reflection prompts, and ways to restart after a missed day.
  • Simple Meal Planner offers four weeks of flexible meal-planning pages and shopping lists.
  • Move at Home is a four-week, no-equipment movement guide with a chair option for every movement.
  • Back on Track is a 14-day guide for returning to simple routines without guilt.
Which languages are available?

Each guide is available in English, French, and Spanish. Choose your language on the product page before purchase so the matching PDF is prepared for you.

What is included in the three-guide bundle?

The Curvafit bundle brings together Gentle Walking, Simple Meal Planner, and Move at Home at the bundle price shown on the offer. Back on Track is included as a complimentary guide. Review the offer details and select the language options before completing your order.

How do I access a digital guide after purchase?

Check your inbox, spam, and promotions folders, then open the confirmation page from your receipt email. If you still need help, contact support with the email used at checkout and your order number. Please do not send your full card details.

No. The guides are digital PDF files; no printed book is shipped. You can read a PDF on a compatible phone, tablet, or computer, or print it for your own use.

Using the guides

Are Curvafit guides medical or individualized nutrition advice?

No. They provide general wellness ideas and planning tools; they do not diagnose or treat a health condition and are not a personalized care plan. Adapt suggestions to your needs, and speak with a qualified professional about personal health questions.

Please consult a healthcare professional before starting. Read our full Medical Disclaimer & Safety.

Do I need equipment for Move at Home, and can I use a chair?

No equipment is needed for the guide’s movement routines. Each movement has a chair variation. Choose an option that feels appropriate for you, pause when needed, and consult a healthcare professional before starting.

What if I miss a walk, workout, or planning day?

You do not need to punish yourself or make up a missed session. Return when you are ready, or repeat a week if that feels more manageable. The guides are meant to support steady habits, not perfect streaks.

Do the guides promise specific health or body results?

No. Individual experiences vary, and Curvafit does not promise weight, calorie, fitness, symptom, or other health outcomes. The guides offer structure and ideas for building routines at your own pace.
