✦ Health & Safety

Medical Disclaimer & Safety

Your wellbeing comes first. Curvafit resources are gentle, general wellness education—not personal medical care.

!

Please consult a healthcare professional before starting any Curvafit movement, walking, or meal-planning guide. Choose someone who understands your health history and can advise you personally.

01 / A clear starting point

What this disclaimer means

Curvafit is here to support steady habits with kindness. Our guides can offer ideas and structure, but they cannot assess what is safe or suitable for you.

01

General education

Walking plans, gentle movement examples, reflection prompts, and meal-planning ideas are provided for general informational purposes.

02

Not medical care

Nothing on this site diagnoses, treats, cures, or prevents a condition, or replaces care from your doctor, registered dietitian, physical therapist, or other qualified professional.

03

No promised outcomes

People and circumstances differ. Curvafit does not promise changes in weight, calories, fitness, symptoms, or any health outcome.

Using a chair option, a shorter session, or a rest day is valid. A guide cannot confirm that a movement is safe for your individual circumstances.

02 / Personal guidance

Before you begin

Ask your healthcare professional what movement and food choices fit your needs. Share the specific guide or activity you plan to try.

+

Please check in first if you:

  • Have a health condition, ongoing symptoms, an injury, or a recent surgery.
  • Have concerns with balance, mobility, joints, heart or breathing, blood pressure, or blood sugar.
  • Are pregnant, recently gave birth, or are returning to activity after a long break.
  • Take medication that may affect activity, heart rate, blood pressure, blood sugar, appetite, or hydration.
  • Are unsure whether a movement or meal idea is appropriate for you.

This list is not complete. If you are uncertain, ask a qualified professional before beginning.

A helpful question to ask
“What is a comfortable and appropriate way for me to begin, and what should I change or avoid?”

Your clinician may suggest adaptations, a different starting point, or waiting until a health concern has been assessed.

03 / Move with care

Gentle movement and exercise safety

Your pace is personal. The aim is a routine that feels manageable—not to push through discomfort or keep up with someone else.

Start where you are

Small, comfortable steps count.

  • Begin gently and build up gradually only when it feels comfortable.
  • Use a sturdy chair or other support when appropriate; clear your space and choose stable footwear.
  • Rest when you need to. Do not punish yourself or try to “make up” a missed session.
  • For many people, moderate activity allows conversation but not singing. This simple talk test is only a general guide; it is not a medical test and may not suit everyone.

Listen to your body

Pause when something feels wrong.

Stop the activity if you develop pain, dizziness, unusual or severe shortness of breath, feel faint, or simply do not feel well. Do not push through warning symptoms. Contact a healthcare professional for guidance before returning.

Using equipment? Follow the manufacturer's instructions, check items for damage, and use them only as intended. Stop if equipment feels unstable or causes pain.
04 / Food without pressure

Meal-planning ideas are not a treatment plan

Curvafit meal-planning content offers general meal ideas and shopping-list inspiration. It is not individualized nutrition care.

Make choices that fit your needs.

If you manage a health condition, have food allergies or intolerances, follow a prescribed diet, are pregnant or breastfeeding, or have a history of disordered eating, speak with your healthcare professional or a registered dietitian before using meal suggestions.

Check ingredients and packaging for allergens, and make substitutions that are safe for you. Do not stop or change medication or prescribed nutrition guidance because of a Curvafit resource.

♡

No food list or meal plan can account for every person's health, culture, budget, access, preferences, or needs.

!
05 / Know when to get help

Your safety comes before a routine

For chest pain or pressure, fainting, severe difficulty breathing, or symptoms that feel life-threatening, stop and contact your local emergency services now. Do not wait for a reply from Curvafit. For new or worsening symptoms that are not an emergency, contact a healthcare professional.
