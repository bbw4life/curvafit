Our Commitment

Your Safety Comes First — Always

Joint-Friendly Workouts Only

No jumping, no running, no high-impact movements. Every exercise was chosen to protect joints carrying 80–130 kg.

Hormone-Conscious Nutrition

Our meal plans account for the hormonal realities of plus-size bodies — insulin sensitivity, cortisol, and estrogen influence on fat distribution.

Zero Crash Diets. Zero Pills.

300–500 calorie deficit maximum. Real food only. No supplements, no detoxes, no products that damage metabolism or liver function.

Gradual, Permanent Progress

0.5–1 kg per week is the target. Faster is not better — it is a warning sign. We build results that last 12 months and beyond.
