1. Ebook Delivery & Order Confirmation

After payment is confirmed, you can download your Curvafit ebook from the Thank You page. Ebooks do not have shipping costs or delivery times. The download is not sent by email.

  • Use the download button on the Thank You page after checkout
  • Your order confirmation email contains your order details; it does not include the ebook download
  • If you cannot access the Thank You page or download, contact Curvafit through the support options on the site and include your order number

Prefer a printed book? Contact Curvafit through the available support options to ask about a physical copy. Printing and shipping would cost extra; we will confirm availability and the total price before you order.

2. Physical Product Shipping

Physical Curvafit products are shipped to the delivery address selected at checkout. The available shipping options and charges depend on the destination.

Once you place an order for a physical product:

  • Processing time may apply before the order is dispatched
  • When tracking is available, you receive the details after dispatch
  • Delivery is handled by the carrier shown for your chosen shipping option

Order processing days: Monday to Friday, excluding public holidays. Orders placed on weekends are processed the next business day.

3. Physical Product Delivery Times

Current estimated delivery times for physical products are:

Shipping Method Estimated Delivery Cost
Standard Shipping 10–20 days Shown at checkout
Faster Shipping 2–10 days Paid; varies by distance
Ebooks Download on the Thank You page after payment No shipping charge

These are estimates and may vary with distance, order processing, customs, and carrier conditions. The shipping method and final delivery estimate available for your address are confirmed at checkout.

4. Shipping Costs

Standard shipping costs and paid faster-shipping rates depend on your address and are shown at checkout before payment. Faster shipping takes an estimated 2–10 days, and its extra cost varies by distance. Ebooks are delivered digitally and have no shipping charge.

Standard Shipping

Estimated delivery in 10–20 days. The rate for your address is shown at checkout.

Express Shipping

Paid faster delivery in an estimated 2–10 days. The additional rate varies by distance and is shown at checkout.

Ebooks

Download your ebook from the Thank You page after payment. No shipping method or shipping fee applies.

Printed Book

Ask Curvafit about a physical copy. Printing and shipping cost extra; availability and price are confirmed before ordering.

5. Order Tracking

Once your physical order is dispatched, you will receive a tracking confirmation email containing your tracking number and a link to follow your package in real time.

  • Tracking updates may take 24–48 hours to appear after dispatch — this is normal and does not indicate a problem
  • Tracking is provided by the carrier handling your shipment (varies by destination and shipping method)
  • If your tracking number shows no updates for more than 7 business days, contact our support team

Tracking issue? Contact Curvafit support with your order number and tracking number so we can help check the shipment status.

6. International Shipping

Curvafit ships physical products to destinations available at checkout. International orders are subject to the following conditions:

  • Shipping availability and costs for your specific country are shown at checkout
  • International delivery times are estimates — customs processing in your country may add additional time
  • Customs authorities, local postal services, and import regulations may affect delivery timing
  • Some products may not be available for shipping to all destinations due to partner supplier restrictions — you will be notified at checkout if this applies

If your country is not listed at checkout or you are unsure about shipping availability to your location, contact us before placing your order: support@bbw4life.com

7. Customs & Import Duties

For international orders, your package may be subject to customs inspection and import duties or taxes levied by your country's customs authority. These charges are not included in your Curvafit order total and may be payable by the recipient.

Charge Type Who Pays? When?
Import duties & taxes Customer (recipient) Upon delivery or customs clearance
Customs inspection delays N/A — handled by customs authorities Variable — depends on destination country
Return-to-sender due to customs refusal Customer responsible for return shipping If package is refused at customs

Check your country's import requirements before ordering. Any duties, taxes, or customs charges are set by local authorities and are separate from Curvafit shipping fees.

8. Lost or Damaged Orders

If a physical order appears lost or arrives damaged, contact Curvafit with your order details so we can review the issue.

Lost Package

  • Contact us if tracking has no updates for more than 7 business days, or if the estimated delivery date has passed by more than 10 business days. The first threshold flags a tracking issue; the second flags a delivery that is significantly overdue.
  • We will open an investigation with the carrier on your behalf
  • If the package is confirmed lost, we will review the available resolution under our Refund Policy and applicable consumer rights

Damaged or Incorrect Item

  • If your item arrives damaged, defective, or incorrect, photograph the packaging and the product immediately before opening or disposing of anything
  • Email us within 7 days of delivery at billing@bbw4life.com with your photos and order number
  • We will review the claim and explain the next steps under our Refund Policy

Damaged, defective, or incorrect items: Contact us within 7 days of delivery, as described in our Refund Policy.

Lost package: support@bbw4life.com

Damaged, defective, or incorrect item: billing@bbw4life.com

9. Incorrect Delivery Address

Please check your delivery address before confirming a physical order. Address changes may not be possible once an order has entered processing or been dispatched.

  • If you realize you entered an incorrect address within 1 hour of placing your order, contact us immediately at support@bbw4life.com — we will do our best to correct it before dispatch
  • Once an order has been dispatched, we cannot change the delivery address
  • If a package is returned to sender due to an incorrect address provided by the customer, reshipping costs are the customer's responsibility
  • If an incorrect address was entered, contact Curvafit support promptly so we can check whether the shipment can still be updated

Address correction window: Contact us within 1 hour of order placement for the best chance of correction. After dispatch, no address changes are possible.

Support email: support@bbw4life.com
