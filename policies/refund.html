1. Our Approach to Refunds

Curvafit offers digital wellness guides and physical movement products. Because a downloadable guide cannot be returned in the same way as a physical item, each type of purchase has its own refund rules, explained below.

If you have a question about what a guide includes, contact us before purchasing. If you have already purchased it and cannot find a topic you expected, our team can help you locate relevant material and, where available, prepare or share a few pages that cover the subject. A content expectation mismatch does not make an ebook refundable, except where applicable law requires otherwise.

For a physical return or refund request, contact our billing team at billing@bbw4life.com with your purchase email and order number. For help finding information in an ebook, use the Curvafit support options on the site.

2. Physical Product Returns

Physical products purchased from Curvafit may be returned under the conditions below. These return terms apply to physical items only; digital guides are covered in Section 6.

Eligibility Conditions

To be eligible for a product return and refund, the following conditions must be met:

  • The item must be returned in its original condition — unused, unwashed, undamaged, and with its original packaging and tags where applicable
  • The return request should be submitted within 30 days of delivery. If exceptional circumstances prevent this, contact us as soon as possible; approval is not guaranteed.
  • You must provide proof of purchase (order confirmation email or order number)

Return Timeline

Step Action Timeline
1 Submit your return request to billing@bbw4life.com As soon as possible after reception
2 Our team reviews your request and sends return instructions Within 5 business days
3 You return the item following the instructions provided Within the delay specified
4 Curvafit reviews the returned item and processes any approved refund Refund initiated within up to 30 days; payment providers may need an additional 3–10 business days

⚠️ Important: Before You Order

To avoid return complications, please take these steps before placing your order:

  • Check your size carefully — consult the size guide on the product page before ordering clothing or footwear. Sizing can vary between suppliers.
  • Verify your delivery address — ensure your shipping address is complete and accurate before confirming your order.

Please check your delivery details before completing your order. Contact Curvafit as soon as possible if you notice an address error.

3. Damaged or Incorrect Item

If a physical product arrives damaged, defective, or different from what you ordered, contact Curvafit so we can review the issue and explain the available resolution.

Photograph Immediately

Before opening or disposing of any packaging, photograph both the packaging and the item clearly. This is essential for your claim.

Contact Within 7 Days

Email us within 7 days of delivery with your photos and order number. Early contact gives us the best options to help you quickly.

Team Review

Our team reviews your photos and order details carefully and responds within 5 business days with a proposed resolution.

Replacement or Refund

If your claim is confirmed, we will arrange an appropriate remedy, which may include a replacement or refund where applicable. We will tell you whether the item needs to be returned.

How to Submit a Damaged or Incorrect Item Claim

  1. Email billing@bbw4life.com with subject line: "Damaged / Incorrect Item"
  2. Include your full name, purchase email, and order number
  3. Attach clear photos of the item and packaging
  4. Briefly describe the issue — what you received and what was wrong
  5. Our team will acknowledge receipt within 2 business days and notify you of the outcome within 5 business days

Note: Please report damaged or incorrect items within 7 days of delivery so we can review the issue promptly. This request does not limit any consumer rights that cannot legally be excluded. Photos submitted for this evaluation are used to assess your claim.

4. Physical Order Cancellation

For physical orders, contact Curvafit promptly if you need to cancel. We can cancel an order before dispatch; after dispatch, use the physical return process in Section 2. Digital guide cancellation requests are handled under applicable law, especially once download access has been provided.

Step What Happens Timeline
1 You request cancellation via email to support@bbw4life.com Before order dispatch only
2 We verify whether the order has already been dispatched Within 1 business day
3 If not yet dispatched, cancellation is confirmed and processing begins Within 1–3 business days
4 Full refund issued to your original payment method Up to 30 days (payment provider dependent)

After Dispatch — No Cancellation Possible

Once your order has been dispatched, cancellation is no longer possible. However, you may initiate a return once the item is received, subject to the conditions in Section 2.

Changing Your Refund Payment Method

If you wish to receive your refund via a different payment method than the one used at purchase, request it before the refund is initiated. Contact billing@bbw4life.com with your request. We cannot modify the payment destination once processing has started.

5. How Refunds Are Processed

Approved physical-product refunds and any refund required by applicable law are handled as follows:

  • Method: Refunds are returned via the same payment method used at purchase (Stripe, PayPal, Visa, Mastercard, American Express, etc.) — unless an alternative method is approved before the refund is initiated
  • Timeline: We initiate approved refunds within up to 30 days after validation. After initiation, your bank or payment provider may take an additional 3–10 business days to show the funds.
  • Currency: Refunds are issued in the same currency as the original transaction
  • Notification: You will receive an email confirmation once your refund has been initiated
  • Bank delay: After we initiate the refund, your bank or payment provider may take an additional 3–10 business days to reflect the amount in your account — this is outside our control

Always include in your refund request: Your purchase email address and your order number. This allows our team to process your request without delays.

Refund email: billing@bbw4life.com

Response time: We acknowledge refund requests within 2 business days. Approved refunds are initiated within up to 30 days after validation; banks or payment providers may need an additional 3–10 business days to post the funds.

6. Digital Guide Refunds and Other Exclusions

Curvafit ebooks are non-refundable once digital access or the download has been provided. This includes purchases where the guide does not contain a topic or detail you expected, or where you decide the material is not what you were looking for. Please read the product description and available preview before purchasing.

If you cannot find information you expected in an ebook, contact us through the support options on the site. We will help you find relevant content and, where available, prepare or share a few pages covering the subject you want to learn more about. This support does not change the ebook refund rule, except where applicable law requires a refund.

The following physical-product situations are also generally not eligible for a return or refund:

  • Physical items that have been used, washed, damaged after delivery, or returned outside the conditions in Section 2
  • Refund requests submitted without required documentation (proof of purchase, photos for damage claims)
  • Requests made after the eligible return window has passed without prior contact
  • Situations where incorrect address information was provided at checkout and delivery failed

Nothing in this policy removes consumer rights that cannot legally be excluded in your location.

7. Tips to Avoid Issues

Review the product details before ordering, and contact us if you need help choosing a guide or understanding what is included:

  • Check product details: Review the product page for materials, features, size information, and what is included.
  • Double-check your delivery address: Verify your full address — street, number, city, country, and postal code — before confirming any order.
  • Keep your confirmation email: Your order number is in your confirmation email. Save it — you will need it for any support request.
  • Inspect physical orders on arrival: Check your items as soon as they arrive and report any damage or discrepancy within 7 days.
  • Review ebook previews: Check the guide description, language, and available sample pages before completing your purchase.
  • Contact us early: If something feels off — with delivery, product quality, or your order — reach out sooner rather than later. Early contact gives us more options to help you.
