1. Who We Are

CurvaFit is a premium feminine fitness brand focused on empowering curvy women through sustainable wellness programs. Our website is www.curvafit.com. For privacy inquiries, contact support@curvafit.com.

2. Information We Collect

Personal Information

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Payment information (processed by third-party providers)
  • Messages submitted through forms

Automatically Collected Information

  • IP address
  • Browser type
  • Device type
  • Pages visited
  • Cookies
  • Analytics data

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide fitness programs
  • To process payments
  • To respond to inquiries
  • To send newsletters (if consent given)

4. Email Communications

Users may receive emails after subscribing. You can unsubscribe anytime. We have a no-spam policy and only send relevant content.

5. Payment Processing

Payments are handled by secure third-party providers. CurvaFit does not store credit card details. Transactions are encrypted.

6. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

We use essential cookies for functionality, analytics cookies for usage insights, and marketing cookies for personalization. Manage settings via your browser. Google Analytics may be used.

7. How We Share Information

We do not sell personal data. Data may be shared with payment processors, email providers, analytics providers, or legal authorities if required.

8. Data Security

We use secure hosting, SSL encryption, and restricted access to protect data.

9. Data Retention

Data is kept as long as necessary. Request deletion anytime.
