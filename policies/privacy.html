1. Who We Are

CurvaFit is a science-based weight loss program designed specifically for plus-size women. We provide structured meal plans, low-impact home workouts, and community support to help women achieve safe, sustainable fat loss — without pills, crash diets, or extreme restriction.

Our website is www.curvafit.com. When you interact with CurvaFit — visiting our site, purchasing a program, joining our community, or contacting our team — you share data with us. This policy tells you exactly how that data is handled.

Data Controller: CurvaFit

Privacy Email: privacy@curvafit.com

General Support: support@curvafit.com

Response time: All privacy requests answered within 30 days (GDPR requirement). Most within 5 business days.

What We Do NOT Collect

We do not collect your phone number (unless you voluntarily include it in a support message). We do not collect your physical address. We do not collect medical records or diagnoses. We do not collect data on your race, religion, or political views.

3. How We Use Your Information

Your data is used for exactly these purposes — and nothing else:

  • Program delivery: Give you access to your purchased program — meal plans, workout videos, progress tracker
  • Support: Read and respond to your questions, technical issues, or billing requests
  • Program emails: Send emails that are part of your program (weekly check-ins, plateau support) — these are not marketing, they are program content
  • Newsletter: Send tips and updates — only if you explicitly subscribed, always with a one-click unsubscribe link
  • Site improvement: Analyze anonymized, aggregated usage data to improve content quality
  • Fraud prevention: Use IP addresses and purchase patterns to detect fraudulent transactions
  • Legal compliance: Retain purchase records for the legally required period (5 years in most jurisdictions)

4. Email Communications

Transactional Emails (Always Sent)

These are necessary to deliver the service you paid for: purchase confirmation, program access, password reset, and support replies. You cannot opt out of these while your account is active — they are part of the service itself.

Marketing & Newsletter Emails (Opt-In Only)

Tips, articles, promotions, and community updates. You receive these only if you explicitly checked the newsletter box during signup or subscribed on our site. Every marketing email contains a visible one-click unsubscribe link. We process unsubscribe requests within 48 hours. We send no more than 2–3 marketing emails per week.

To unsubscribe or change your email preferences: privacy@curvafit.com

5. Payment Processing

CurvaFit does not store, process, or have access to your credit card or bank account details. All payments are processed by Stripe — a PCI DSS Level 1 certified payment processor, the highest security standard in the payments industry.

When you enter payment information, it goes directly to Stripe's secure servers. CurvaFit receives only a transaction confirmation and the last 4 digits of your card for your records. Your full card details never pass through or are stored on CurvaFit systems.

Stripe's privacy policy: stripe.com/privacy

Refund requests: billing@curvafit.com — 30-day money-back guarantee

Include: Your purchase email and order number for faster processing

6. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

CurvaFit uses cookies — small text files stored on your device — to make the site function and to understand how it is used:

Cookie Type Purpose Can You Disable?
Essential cookies Keep you logged in, remember your cart, enable checkout — the site cannot function without these No (required for function)
Analytics cookies Google Analytics — how many visitors, which pages are most read, session duration. Fully anonymized. No individual user is tracked. Yes — browser settings or Google Analytics opt-out add-on
Marketing cookies Only if you have explicitly consented — allow us to show relevant content and measure performance Yes — cookie banner or browser settings

How to Disable Cookies

Browser settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies. You can also install the Google Analytics Opt-Out Add-On. Note: disabling essential cookies will prevent login and checkout from working.

8. Data Security

We take the following concrete measures to protect your data:

  • SSL encryption: All data transmitted between your browser and our servers is encrypted via HTTPS/SSL. The padlock in your browser confirms this.
  • Secure hosting: Our servers are hosted with providers that maintain physical and digital security including firewalls, intrusion detection, and regular audits.
  • Access control: Only team members who need your data for their specific role can access it. A support agent does not have access to your billing history. A billing agent does not have access to your health data.
  • Password hashing: Passwords are never stored in plain text. They are hashed using one-way encryption — CurvaFit cannot see your password.
  • Health data isolation: Your weight, measurements, and progress data are stored separately from your account and billing data and are never used for marketing analysis.

No system is 100% secure. In the unlikely event of a data breach affecting your personal information, we will notify you and the relevant authorities within 72 hours as required by GDPR.

10. Your Rights Under GDPR

If you are located in the European Union, United Kingdom, or any jurisdiction with equivalent data protection laws, you have the following rights over your personal data. These rights are real, actionable, and free to exercise:

Right of Access

Request a copy of all personal data CurvaFit holds about you. We provide it in a readable format within 30 days.

Right of Rectification

Request correction of any inaccurate or incomplete data we hold. We update it within 30 days.

Right to Erasure

Request complete deletion of your data ("right to be forgotten"). We delete all non-legally-required data within 30 days.

Right to Portability

Request your data in a structured, machine-readable format (CSV or JSON) so you can transfer it elsewhere.

Right to Object

Object to any specific use of your data — including marketing emails, analytics, or profiling. We honor your objection immediately.

Right to Restriction

Limit how we use your data without deleting it — for example, keep your account but stop all marketing use.

How to Exercise Your Rights

Email privacy@curvafit.com with your full name, the email address on your account, and a description of the right you wish to exercise. We confirm receipt within 48 hours and fulfill the request within 30 days.

If you believe we have not handled your data correctly, you have the right to lodge a complaint with your national data protection authority (e.g. ICO in the UK, CNIL in France, or the equivalent in your country).

12. Children's Privacy

CurvaFit's programs are designed for adults aged 18 and above. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18. If you are under 18, do not use our services or submit any personal information. If we become aware that we have inadvertently collected data from a minor, we will delete it immediately. To report this: privacy@curvafit.com.
